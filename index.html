ABOUT US

We, Sanpravahi (Sand Painting), would like to introduce to you a unique creation made from underground natural coloured stone powder, sea sand and river sand.

These collections are made using 100% natural colourful stones and sand collected from various places. Those stones are ground into powder, which becomes the raw material for our pure handmade masterpieces.

Our creations include Black Sand Art and Multi-Colour Sand Art, showcasing the beauty of natural materials through handmade artistic creations.


ARTIST | SANU K MOHAN

I am a professional Sand Artist from Kerala and the Founder of Sanpravahi Sand Art Gallery — a creative and socially driven art initiative dedicated to transforming natural sand into meaningful works of art.

With a deep passion for creativity and cultural expression, I specialize in customized sand portraits, artistic wall frames, home décor, mementos, live sand art performances, and professional sand art training programs. My work focuses on turning simple grains of sand into powerful visual stories that connect emotionally with audiences.

Beyond artistic excellence, my mission is rooted in social impact. Through Sanpravahi, I empower rural women in Kerala by providing sand art skill training and sustainable income opportunities. I also support differently-abled individuals by helping them develop artistic skills and achieve financial independence with dignity.
