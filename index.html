ABOUT US

We, Sanpravahi (Sand Painting), would like to introduce to you a unique creation made from underground natural coloured stone powder, sea sand and river sand.

These collections are made using 100% natural colourful stones and sand collected from various places. Those stones are ground into powder, which becomes the raw material for our pure handmade masterpieces.

Our creations include Black Sand Art and Multi-Colour Sand Art, showcasing the beauty of natural materials through handmade artistic creations.
